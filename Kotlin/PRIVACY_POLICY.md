# Privacy Policy --- INFINITY Focus Reminder

**Effective date:** 16 November 2026\
**App version covered:** Original / old APK (the build without login and
the game-style leaderboard)\
**Developer:** INFINITYMinds

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies only to the original INFINITY Focus Reminder
APK distributed through the INFINITYMinds website or GitHub.

It does **not** apply to the later Firebase-based APK that includes a
login screen and a game-style leaderboard. That later build is currently
non-functional and may remain stuck on its login screen. Users should
download the original APK instead.

## 2. Information collected by the app developer

The original app does not have an app backend or connect to a
developer-operated server. The developer does not collect users' email
addresses or send users' personal information to an INFINITYMinds
server.

The app is designed to work offline for its normal on-device features.
No user account or login is required.

## 3. Accessibility Service and on-device app detection

The app uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect which app is
currently open so that its app-blocking feature can work.

This information is processed on the device for that feature. According
to the developer, it is not sent to an INFINITYMinds server or stored in
a developer-operated backend.

The Accessibility Service is a sensitive Android permission. Users
should review the Android permission prompt and enable it only if they
understand and want the app-blocking functionality.

## 4. Smart Shield and third-party DNS

Smart Shield uses CleanBrowsing DNS filtering to help block adult
websites.

When a device uses a third-party DNS resolver, DNS requests may be sent
to that provider. INFINITYMinds does not operate that DNS service and
cannot confirm what information the provider processes, logs, or
retains. This policy therefore does not claim that the third-party DNS
provider collects no information.

For information about the provider's own practices, users should consult
CleanBrowsing's current privacy documentation.

## 5. Android permissions and settings

Depending on the device and Android version, the app may ask users to
enable permissions or settings such as:

-   Accessibility Service, for detecting the currently open app and
    supporting app blocking.
-   Auto-start, where the device manufacturer provides this setting.
-   Battery optimisation or background-running exemptions, to help the
    app continue its intended functions.

These settings are controlled by Android and/or the device manufacturer.
The app uses them for its stated functionality. Users should review each
permission and decide whether to enable it.

## 6. Firebase, accounts, analytics and advertising

The original APK covered by this policy does not use the later build's
Firebase login or game-style leaderboard. It does not require an
account. The developer has stated that this original build has no app
backend and does not use developer-operated analytics or advertising
services.

## 7. Data security and deletion

Because the developer does not receive user personal information through
an app backend, the developer does not hold an account database for this
original build from which to retrieve or delete user profiles.

Any information or settings maintained locally by Android or on the
device are under the device user's control. Uninstalling the app may
remove app-local data, subject to Android and device behaviour.

Third-party DNS processing, if any, is governed by the DNS provider's
own practices.

## 8. Children's privacy

The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information
through this original APK. The app should be installed and configured
with the knowledge and permission of a parent or guardian where required
by applicable law or family rules.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If the app's functionality, permissions, or data practices change, this
policy may be updated. The latest version should be published alongside
the APK download.

## 10. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact INFINITYMinds through the
official contact method published on the INFINITYMinds website.
